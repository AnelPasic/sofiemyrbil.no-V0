## Non-negotiable standards

1. Norsk klarhet: a real buyer understands what is offered and the practical outcome immediately.
2. Kommersiell logikk: connect action to a meaningful customer decision or consequence, not empty agency descriptions.
3. Honesty and evidence: no invented facts, rankings, reviews, guarantees, claims, AI mentions, prices or proof.
4. SEO/AIO and CRO: content must answer its search/buyer question and support an appropriate, truthful next action.
5. No forced jokes; reject humour that obscures the answer or disrespects the buyer.

## Supported presets

- **Nøktern (noktern):** plain, precise, unambiguous. For facts, prices, proof, policies, offers and form/error controls.
- **Balansert (balansert):** direct Norwegian explanation with a real buyer situation and commercial relevance. Suggested default for NEW customer sites; OWNER chooses final.
- **Spiss (spiss, level 3):** one sharp dry observation if it strengthens comprehension. Do not make every paragraph snarky.
- **Spiss+ (spissplus, level 4):** a clearer contrast and a memorable, concrete analogy illustrating a genuine commercial failure mode. More insight, not more jokes or noise.

For this brand, the OWNER can choose different values. Do not copy another client's brand personality or Spiss+ setting. The preset level is an editorial label, not a numeric claim or a 0-3 per-dimension intensity. There is no universal “one joke per FAQ”.

## How to apply

- Hero: clarify who/what/value/action; restrain edge.
- Services/sales: real buyer outcome, relevant scope, correct promises.
- Educational/explainer: when fitting, use vivid real examples and a dry observation to make the lesson stick.
- FAQ: answer first, then optional sharp observation; keep actual policy/price/scope accurate.
- Proof, prices, legal, privacy, medical, safety, accessibility, checkout/form errors: nøktern regardless of selected profile.

To apply a saved config: read fresh JSON, choose the field for the requested section, resolve inherit against defaultPreset, then apply the installed Content Quality Gate risk lane A/B/C and show before/after or write only specifically authorized CMS fields. ## Original example calibration - do not publish automatically

Question: "Hvorfor får vi mange besøk, men få henvendelser?"

- Nøktern: "Vi undersøker hvilke besøk som er relevante, hvordan tilbudet presenteres og om det er enkelt å ta kontakt."
- Balansert: "Da undersøker vi om besøkene er relevante, om tilbudet er tydelig og om folk finner kontaktveien."
- Spiss: "Det hjelper lite at mange finner nettsiden dersom de ikke finner ut hvordan de skal kjøpe."
- Spiss+: "Flere besøk løser lite hvis kontaktskjemaet ber om en selvbiografi før kunden får stille et spørsmål. Først må vi finne ut hvor interessen stopper."

These are illustrations, **not evidence about the client's actual funnel**. Use only with appropriate context.
